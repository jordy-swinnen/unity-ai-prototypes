# Audio credits

All sound effects and music in this repository are generated by scripts in `Tools/audio/` from nothing: no samples, no recordings. The only third-party audio input is the SoundFont the music renderer uses at build time.

## SoundFont

**GeneralUser GS 2.0.3** by S. Christian Collins.

- Source: <https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser> (mirror: <https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS>)
- License text read in full on 2026-11-11 from the author's repository (`documentation/LICENSE.txt`, v2.0.3):
  - "You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own music creation, private or commercial. … Please feel free to use it in your software projects, and to modify the SoundFont bank or its packaging to suit your needs."
  - Samples: "all of which allow full use in music production, including the ability to make profit from musical recordings created with GeneralUser GS."
  - The author notes he cannot be 100% sure where every sample originated (the bank predates 2000), knows of no complaint since then, and asks to be told of any restricted sample.
- Used as a **build-time tool input** only. The `.sf2` is committed under Git LFS at `Tools/audio/soundfonts/GeneralUser-GS.sf2` and is not shipped as a runtime asset; the game plays the rendered `.wav` files.
- Instruments used so far: Marimba (GM 12) and Music Box (GM 10).

## Rendered music

- `Assets/Prototypes/PixelInvaders/Audio/music.wav` — "Trench Pulse", composed in `Tools/audio/PixelInvaders/music/trench_pulse.py` (Electric Piano 2 GM 5, Synth Bass 1 GM 38, Pad 2 Warm GM 89), rendered with FluidSynth 2.6.2 and GeneralUser GS 2.0.3.
- `Assets/Prototypes/MedievalChess/Audio/music.wav` — "Torchlight Hall", composed in `Tools/audio/MedievalChess/music/torchlight.py` (Acoustic Guitar Nylon GM 24, Orchestral Harp GM 46, Cello GM 42), rendered with FluidSynth 2.6.2 and GeneralUser GS 2.0.3.
- `Assets/Prototypes/NeonPong/Audio/music.wav` — "Neon Drift", composed in `Tools/audio/NeonPong/music/neon_drift.py` (Electric Piano 2 GM 5, Synth Bass 1 GM 38, Pad 2 Warm GM 89), rendered with FluidSynth 2.6.2 and GeneralUser GS 2.0.3.
